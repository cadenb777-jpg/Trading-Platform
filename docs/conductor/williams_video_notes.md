# Owner-supplied transcript: Larry Williams' Bloomberg indicators (YouTube D9Q4S0YhO3k, recorded April 15 2013)

Segments and what he says (paraphrased, timestamps from the transcript):

- 0:00-4:27 Acquisition Index. From his COT work since 1973 he found how commercials acquire positions and built a
  formula that "emulates their buying and selling pressures", applied to stocks (and futures). Shown on WEEKLY
  charts (IBM, Google, GE, gold, bonds). High index = under acquisition by large/smart money -> expect substantial
  intermediate-term rallies (not 1-2 day moves); low = no acquisition / distribution -> declines. "It doesn't matter
  if price level is high or low." Purpose: a SETUP to "improve your timing" and make sure large funds are buying with
  you. Formula not disclosed in the video.
- 4:27-7:38 Cycle Forecast (projects 3 months ahead). "Obviously not perfect". Proprietary.
- 7:38-11:22 Institutional accumulation index = his "new and improved version of unbalanced volume" (first built
  1966). Key use is DIVERGENCE: accumulation making new highs / breaking out above its prior peak while price is
  still flat in a trading range = bullish (Apple run-up, soybeans 2012, gold, IBM base); price making a new high but
  accumulation not (no follow-through) = distribution, expect a decline (Apple top, gold 2013, IBM Oct 2012).
- 11:22-15:45 LW Sentiment Index (advisor bullish %, contrarian; first in his 1999 Bloomberg book). "Not a license
  to buy": once set up, "apply your short-term trading tools, your trend-following tool to get into the market".
- 15:45-21:34 True Seasonal: seasonal pattern built only from data known up to each point in time (no look-ahead);
  also compare price vs the seasonal (stronger than seasonal into a seasonal buy = strong signal).
- 21:34-25:18 WillTrend: trend line from "a combination of trend and volatility, market expansions"; above it take
  only short-term buy signals, below it only short-term sells; any time frame; "no special rules"; formula not given.

Common structure across all of it: a weekly SETUP (acquisition / accumulation / sentiment / seasonal) tells you a
substantial move is likely and in which direction; a TREND tool keeps you on the right side; a SHORT-TERM trigger
times the entry.
