# North star: what this platform is for

Written from everything the owner has asked for, so every review judges against the same goal.

## The goal in one line

A personal Bloomberg for a one-person RIA: everything the owner needs to watch markets, research a company, test
an idea, trade carefully and serve clients, in one place, with free data, that looks like it belongs on a stage at a
tech summit and is still easy enough for an older client to use.

## Who uses it

- **The owner** (a beginner trader and an RIA): daily market check, research, setups, trading through Webull,
  client reports. Explanations in plain language; numbers with their source and date.
- **Clients and older users**: large clear text, obvious buttons, nothing hidden behind hover only, no dead ends.

## What "done" looks like (the review checklist)

1. **Works every time.** Every button does what its label says; no console errors; no blank screens without a
   reason and a next step; nothing overlaps or gets cut off at laptop or phone size; both themes.
2. **Loops.** From any stock: overview, chart, financials, options, news, research, trade ticket, alerts, and back,
   without dead ends. The command bar takes tickers, Bloomberg-style codes and page names.
3. **Trustworthy numbers.** Every figure shows source and as-of; institutional methods (mid-year DCF, comps on
   EV/EBITDA first, after-cost backtests, no look-ahead); green and red only where they mean better or worse.
4. **Bloomberg feel, the owner's style.** Amber labels, white figures, green/red changes; dark navy with the slow
   flowing blue and orange background; rounded depth cards; square dotted grid on price charts only; calm motion.
5. **Simple to use.** Clear page titles, one main thing per page, short labels, explanations behind the "i", the
   function bar with numbered keys, the menu listing every page.
6. **Safe.** Secrets stay in the helper; orders only through preview and confirm with Test first; the AI never
   trades; prediction markets read-only.
7. **Personal touches the owner chose.** Bull the pixel market buddy (stays out of the way, never on the Chart page),
   the ticker tape, the 12-hour clock.

## What not to change without asking

The colour scheme and background, the menu layout, the chart grid style, Bull, the ticker tape, the page order. The
owner likes the current site; reviews fix and polish, they do not redesign.
